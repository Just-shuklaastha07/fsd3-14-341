Our story

Old craft.
New paths.

Sutrā began with a simple question: what if the craft we treasure could walk with us every day? We turn heritage textiles into expressive, comfortable sneakers—without losing the human hand behind them.

Why Sutrā

Craft should be lived in.

Across India, generations of makers have mastered embroidery, resist printing and hand weaving. Yet these extraordinary skills are too often kept for special occasions—or separated from the people who made them.

We work directly with independent artisan groups to reinterpret regional techniques for modern footwear. The process stays slow, the details stay personal, and every collaboration begins with respect.

“We don’t put tradition behind glass. We give it somewhere new to go.”
